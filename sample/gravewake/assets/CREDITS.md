# Original procedural art

All assets used by this version of Gravewake are generated by its own Python and
Dream source: terrain, rocks, trees, cabins, bridge, clouds, supplies, zombies,
weapons, arms and palette colors. There are no downloaded models or image textures
in the runtime asset set. The reference pictures guided broad visual direction;
their images and meshes are not bundled.

Blender evaluates geometry and Three.js / three-mesh-bvh prepare spatial ordering.
Their dependencies retain their own licenses. Earlier downloaded development art
is retained only in the ignored repository asset cache and is not shipped by this
sample. No Sketchfab API credential is needed to rebuild the art.

Audio in `src/input/audio*.js`, including the 108 BPM F# minor synthwave score,
is original synthesized noise and oscillator sound,
created for this demo. No third-party recordings, sound packs or music are used;
there are no external audio royalties or attribution requirements.
